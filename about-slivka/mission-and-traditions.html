Who We Are

Mission

As a Residential College, Slivka is more than just a dorm: it's a vibrant and active community for 140 residents. Slivka hosts dozens of events per academic quarter, ranging from fun activities centered around crafting, games, and community building, to informative events regarding picking classes, undergraduate research, and how to dress for Chicago winters. Furthermore, Slivka has over 15 fellows, who are professors and graduate students affiliated with Slivka, that students can talk to and learn from at many events hosted throughout the quarter. Additionally, the resources we get as a res college allow us to maintain a collection of items for residents to borrow, including cleaning supplies, cookware, and office supplies.

The multitude of events Slivka hosts per quarter nurture an open and familiar culture within Slivka – our community is passionate, caring, and tightly-knit. Many of the people new Slivkans meet within their first few weeks in the dorm become their close friends for all four years of college and beyond. A large part of what makes this community possible is our wonderful executive board, made up of over 10 positions that held by elected Slivkans; together, these elected students are in charge of managing all of Slivka's res college operations and events.

How to Join

If you are an incoming student who is excited to join this community but has yet to fill out their housing contract, then you can apply to live in Slivka through your housing contract! If you have already filled out your housing contract or are a returning student, it is unfortunately too late for you to live in Slivka, but there is still a way for you to join our community. Anyone not living in the building but is interested in joining Slivka can apply to be a non-res (non-resident member) through the form listed on this website. Regardless of what option you choose, there is a membership fee that will be charged to your account once you officially become a part of Slivka.

What We Do

Community and Culture

The culture of Slivka is one of passionate interests and enthusiastic participation. Residents have set up weekly Magic the Gathering play sessions, have debated the best way to solve a rubix cube, and have nerded out about early 2000s fandom culture. Our community is filled with people who care, participate, and think deeply, and we apply those traits to solve problems – whether those be serious problems in real life or entirely theoretical problems made up for fun. We value a culture that allows people to be their real selves and share their true interests, whatever those may be. While the community is composed of many engineers, you do not have to be a part of engineering or STEM to fit within our community.

So Many Events, so Much Fun!

If it wasn't already clear, Slivka hosts SO MANY events! We host more events than every other residental college combined – and this isn't just to make our stats look good, we genuinely have this many fun activities to share with our community. The sheer magnitude of events we host means you are almost guaranteed to find something to enjoy. Whether you like doing fiber crafts, playing Nintendo switch games, competing in trivia tournaments, participating in PowerPoint nights, going on adventures throughout campus, or playing intermural sports, there is something for everyone.
